Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need an entry permit for Lakshadweep?

Yes. All non-residents require an authorized Lakshadweep Entry Permit issued by the Lakshadweep Administration. When enquiring via NOODY WhatsApp, our verified local hosts will guide you through the digital e-Permit process.

2. How does the WhatsApp enquiry process work?

When you click "Enquire on WhatsApp", a pre-formatted message with your chosen island and service ID is generated. NOODY's operational team confirms slot availability, tidal schedules, equipment safety, and final pricing before you commit.

3. What happens if water sports are cancelled due to weather?

Safety is paramount. If port authorities issue wind or surge advisories, outdoor lagoon activities are either rescheduled to a calmer session or completely refunded according to our Weather Cancellation Policy.

4. Are digital payments accepted on the islands?

UPI and digital transfers work in Agatti and Kavaratti where mobile connectivity is strong. However, carrying adequate Indian Rupee cash is advised for remote lagoons and small coconut kiosks.
