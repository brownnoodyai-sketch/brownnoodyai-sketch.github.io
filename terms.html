1. Operational Scope

The NOODY.AI public website serves exclusively as an information and WhatsApp enquiry dispatch layer. All commercial agreements, booking confirmations, service delivery terms, and receipt issuance are governed directly through NOODY Core operations and official Meta WhatsApp Business channels.

2. Island Permit Compliance

Access to Lakshadweep Union Territory is restricted by government statute. Travelers are required to hold a valid Lakshadweep Entry Permit. The website information does not substitute for official statutory permit verification.

3. Water Sports & Safety Protocol

All water sports listed are delivered by certified third-party island operators. Participants must adhere to safety briefings, wear mandatory life jackets, and disclose relevant medical conditions prior to scuba diving or open lagoon activities.

4. Pricing and Payment Transparency

Listed prices represent guide rates. Final quotes, tidal scheduling, and confirmation receipts are delivered via official WhatsApp before payment.
