DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Zero tracking pixels, zero selling of customer data, and secure direct WhatsApp communication.

1. Client-Side Privacy

The NOODY.AI public website does not employ intrusive third-party behavioral trackers or advertising cookies. Your selected island and language preferences are stored purely in your local browser storage (`localStorage`) to preserve your experience across page reloads.

2. WhatsApp Communication

When you initiate an enquiry, your message is transferred securely to the official Meta WhatsApp Business application. WhatsApp communications are protected under Meta's encryption protocols and NOODY's internal operational security standards.

3. Vendor Data Security

Unlisted vendor registration data, identification documents, and phone numbers are stored exclusively in restricted administrative tables and are never exposed publicly on the website or via public REST API endpoints.

4. Feedback Moderation

When you submit reviews, only your provided first name, rating, and feedback comment will be displayed publicly following moderator review. Your email or contact telephone will never be published.
