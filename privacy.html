Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NOODY.AI collects, uses, stores, and shares information when customers, vendors, staff, or visitors use our website, WhatsApp-based services, booking and order tools, vendor services, and related platform features.

1. Information we may collect

  • Contact information such as name, phone number, WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Booking, order, service, product, complaint, refund, support, and transaction-related information.
  • Location information when a user voluntarily shares location for a service that requires it.
  • Feedback, ratings, comments, and photos submitted by users.
  • Vendor profile, business, licence, permit, KYC, banking or settlement-related information where required for onboarding and operations.
  • Technical and security information such as timestamps, device/session details, audit events, and service logs.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide enquiries, bookings, orders, vendor routing, customer support, service notifications, payments, refunds, completion verification, settlement, fraud prevention, security, analytics, legal compliance, and platform improvement.

3. WhatsApp and Meta services

NOODY.AI uses the official Meta WhatsApp Business Platform for operational messaging. Messages and related delivery information may be processed by Meta according to Meta's own terms and privacy practices. We use WhatsApp information only for legitimate platform operations, customer service, booking/order flows, vendor coordination, and permitted notifications.

4. Payments

Payment processing may be provided by third-party payment providers such as Razorpay. Payment credentials such as full card or UPI authentication details are handled by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored by NOODY.AI. We may retain payment references, status, amounts, refund references, and ledger records required for platform operations and accounting.

5. Sharing of information

We may share only the information necessary to fulfil a service with relevant vendors, service providers, payment processors, messaging providers, hosting providers, security providers, or authorities where legally required. We do not sell personal information.

6. Vendor and customer privacy

Vendor information that is not required for the public customer experience is kept for internal operational use. Customer information is shared with vendors only as reasonably necessary to fulfil an accepted order or service.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, legal, accounting, security, dispute, audit, and operational purposes. We use access controls, authentication, logging, encryption where appropriate, and other security measures to protect platform data. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and data deletion requests

You may request correction or deletion of eligible personal information, or ask questions about your data, by contacting NOODY.AI through our official WhatsApp number below. Some records may need to be retained where required for legal, financial, fraud-prevention, dispute, or security purposes.

9. Children
