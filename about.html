Next-gen Oceanic Operations, Data & Yield.

Who We Are

NOODY.AI is an integrated oceanic platform created to empower sustainable island tourism and local business operations across Lakshadweep. By combining modern digital interfaces with official Meta WhatsApp Business connectivity, we connect travelers directly with certified local island operators while ensuring zero exploitation of island resources.

Our Guiding Principles

  • Island-First Authority: Inventory, safety regulations, and weather permits are strictly verified before listing. Agatti operates as our Phase 1 operational hub.
  • Direct Community Empowerment: Eliminating unnecessary intermediaries ensures that payments flow directly to native boat captains, dive guides, homestay families, and women cooperatives.
  • Strict Data Integrity: The public website operates purely as a discovery and customer enquiry layer, maintaining strict operational isolation from the central Core Software backend.
  • Ecological Preservation: We support Lakshadweep's zero single-use plastics mandate and promote responsible, reef-safe diving and snorkeling practices.
