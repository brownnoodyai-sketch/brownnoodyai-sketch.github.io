Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear rules protecting travelers and local island operators during unexpected oceanic conditions.

1. Weather and Port Advisories

Lakshadweep island activities depend directly on sea conditions and official port warnings. If an activity is called off due to rough swells, squalls, or official coast guard safety advisories, customers are entitled to a 100% full refund or complimentary rescheduling to another session.

2. Customer-Initiated Cancellations

  • More than 48 hours prior to scheduled session: 100% refund minus minimal banking processing fee.
  • 24 to 48 hours prior: 50% refund to offset fuel preparation and boat crew staging.
  • Less than 24 hours / No-show: Non-refundable, as equipment and guides are exclusively reserved.

3. Island Products Delivery & Damages

Smoked tuna mas, pickles, and crafts are vacuum-sealed and inspected prior to dispatch. If a package arrives damaged or unsealed during island transit, please submit a photo within 24 hours over WhatsApp for immediate replacement or full refund.
