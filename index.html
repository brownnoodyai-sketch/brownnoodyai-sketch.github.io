A few things to know.

Do I need to sign in?⌄

No. Browse islands, services and products without creating a customer account. Enquiries take place on WhatsApp.

Does tapping WhatsApp confirm a booking?⌄

No. WhatsApp opens with a prepared message. You choose whether to send it. Availability, price and arrangements must be confirmed in the chat.

Why are some services different by island?⌄

Only items assigned to the selected island are shown. Listings can differ according to island availability and operating partners.

Will vendor names appear publicly?⌄

No. Vendor operational information stays private. Customers interact with NOODY.AI and are routed through the official WhatsApp service flow.
